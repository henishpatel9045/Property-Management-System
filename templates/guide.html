Mastering PropRMS

A step-by-step guide to automating your property portfolio.

Introduction

PropRMS is designed to remove the manual burden of property management. By following this guide, you will set up a system that automatically tracks rent, manages documents, and communicates with your tenants.

1 Google Drive Integration

To ensure your data remains under your control, PropRMS uses your personal Google Drive for document storage. This is a mandatory step that you will be prompted to complete the first time you attempt to access your dashboard.

First-Time Setup:
  1. Upon your first login, you will be redirected to the Google Authorization page.
  2. Sign in with your preferred Google Account.
  3. Grant permission for drive.file access.
  4. Once authorized, you will be granted full access to the PropRMS dashboard.

Privacy Note: We only access folders and files created by this app. Your personal Drive content remains strictly private.

2 Property Setup

Properties act as "containers" for your tenants and financial records. To add a property:

  • Navigate to Properties > Add Property.
  • Enter a Nickname (e.g., "Unit 42 - Skyline") and the Full Address.
  • Add optional notes for your reference.

3 Tenant Onboarding

Adding a tenant correctly is vital for automated communications and Telegram linking.

Crucial: Phone numbers MUST include the country code. For Australia, start with +61 (e.g., +61400000000).
Steps:
  1. Go to Tenants > Add Tenant.
  2. Enter their full name and a valid email address.
  3. Enter their Mobile Number in international format.

4 Linking Telegram Reminders

Automate your reminders by linking your tenants to the PropRMS Telegram Bot.

The 5-Step Linking Flow:
Share the Bot: Share this link with your tenant: https://t.me/PropRMSBot
Tenant Start: The tenant opens the bot and clicks the Start button.
Identity Verification: The tenant clicks the "Share My Contact" button within the bot.
Automatic Matching: PropRMS looks at the shared number. If it matches the +61... number you entered in Step 3, the account is linked!
Confirmation: The tenant receives a "Successfully Linked" message and will now get automated rent reminders.

5 Creating Leases

The Lease connects a Tenant to a Property and sets the financial rules.

  • Rent Amount & Frequency: Set how much and how often (Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly).
  • Due Day: Specify the day (e.g., "1" for the 1st of the month, or "1" for Monday).
  • Document Upload: Drag and drop the signed PDF. It will be instantly filed in your Google Drive.

6 Financials & Data Export

PropRMS allows you to take your data with you. You can track all income and expenses and export them for your accountant.

Excel Export

Navigate to the Rent Ledger and click "Export to Excel" to download a full history.

CSV Export

In the Financial Records list, click "Export CSV" for categorized income/expenses.

Revert Entry

Use the "Revert" button to undo an entry. If it's a rent payment, it will also automatically update the Rent Ledger.

7 Understanding the Ledger

The Ledger is where you track the "Health" of your property. Each row represents a rent period. You can:

  • Mark Paid: Record a payment. It will automatically update the tenant's balance.
  • Adjustments: If you give a discount or add a fee, use the "Adjust" feature.
  • Revert: Made a mistake? Click Revert to undo the payment and restore the arrears.
