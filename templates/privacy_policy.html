Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

1. Introduction

Welcome to PropertyMaps RMS ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice or our practices with regard to your personal information, please contact us at henish.patel.au@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and distribute your information, including Personal Data used to access our Rent Management System. In particular, this Privacy Policy applies to the use of Google Cloud OAuth and Google Drive API integrations provided within PropertyMaps RMS.

2. Integration with Google APIs

PropertyMaps RMS uses Google's Application Programming Interface (API) Services to synchronize and store property management documents directly to your Google Drive. Our integration includes the use of Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate your identity securely.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PropertyMaps RMS's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Information We Collect

When you consent to authenticate with Google OAuth, we access the following information:

  • Basic Profile Information: Your name and email address to identify you within our system.
  • Google Drive Permissions: We utilize the auth.drive.file scope, which grants PropertyMaps RMS the ability to see, edit, create, and delete ONLY the specific files and folders that you use with this app. We cannot access your personal photos, documents, or any other files in your Google Drive that were not created or opened by PropertyMaps RMS.

We do not store your Google Drive files on our servers. All document operations happen dynamically and utilize your own Google Drive storage.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect via Google APIs solely to provide you with the core functionality of PropertyMaps RMS:

  • To programmatically create organized folder hierarchies (e.g., Property Maps -> Property Name -> Tenant Name) in your Google Drive.
  • To upload lease agreements, expense receipts, and tenant documents securely into your storage.
  • To provide embedded links within the web app so you can easily access your generated files.

We will never use, sell, or rent your Google user data for advertising purposes or third-party tracking. Access tokens are securely utilized only for backend integration processing as explicitly triggered by your actions.

5. Data Retention & Revocation

You can revoke PropertyMaps RMS's access to your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Account settings (Security -> Third-party apps with account access). Upon revocation, PropertyMaps RMS will immediately lose the ability to sync documents, and no further Google data will be processed.

6. Security Methods

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Access tokens for Google OAuth are stored securely using industry-standard encryptions and are communicated exclusively over HTTPS secure connections.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may email us at henish.patel.au@gmail.com.
